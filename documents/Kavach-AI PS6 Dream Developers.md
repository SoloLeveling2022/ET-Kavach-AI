### 📌 Slide Header

```
  50Hz Gyro Data  ──► [Butterworth Filter]  ──► [Welford Stress] ──► Involuntary Tremor Score
```

### 🔬 Technical & Mathematical Breakthroughs

#### 1. Phoneme-Agent (Glottal Spectral Residual Analysis)
Extracted using Levinson-Durbin autoregressive pole estimation to isolate glottal excitation residual $e(t)$. Frame-by-frame cosine similarity of Wav2Vec 2.0 transformer hidden layers ($H_t$) detects vocoder-induced phonetic drift:
$$\Phi = \frac{1}{T-1} \sum_{t=1}^{T-1} \frac{\langle H_t, H_{t+1} \rangle}{\Vert{}H_t\Vert{}_2 \Vert{}H_{t+1}\Vert{}_2}$$

#### 2. Veritas-Agent (Skin Surface Reflectance Variance)
Calculates surface reflectance variance ($\sigma^2_R$) across 468 facial mesh landmarks to block virtual camera injection drivers (OBS / v4l2loopback):
$$I(x,y) = L(x,y) \cdot R(x,y) \implies \sigma^2_R = \frac{1}{\vert{}\Omega\vert{}} \sum_{(x,y) \in \Omega} (R(x,y) - \bar{R})^2$$

#### 3. Aegis-Agent (Biomechanical Duress Telemetry)
Processes 50Hz accelerometer/gyroscope coordinates through a 2nd-order Butterworth high-pass filter ($y[n]$) to detect involuntary micro-tremors in victims held under extreme psychological stress:
$$y[n] = b_0 x[n] + b_1 x[n-1] + b_2 x[n-2] - a_1 y[n-1] - a_2 y[n-2]$$

#### 4. Hermes-Agent (Multi-Channel Regional Citizen Shield)
Conversational AI walking citizens through fraud verification via WhatsApp, IVR, and PWA in 12 Indian regional languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, English).

### 💡 PM-Level Value Proposition
"By running lightweight quantized models directly in the user browser or edge server, we achieve sub-100ms detection without needing expensive GPU infrastructure. If a scammer uses a deepfake voice or synthetic video, the system alerts the victim and notifies law enforcement before financial transfer occurs."
